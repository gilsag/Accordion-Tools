La Melodiosa — Version 1.0

Sampling, editing and instrument programming:
Petri Pohjanmies (Finland), 2026.

Original sample pack licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY).

These files are distributed with Accordion-Tools and used as the sample source
for its La Melodiosa accordion sound engine. The browser implementation follows
the mappings, register selections, tuning offsets, relative levels, and sample
alternation defined in the supplied SFZ instrument.

The author's original README is retained as README-original.txt. The supplied
SFZ and Decent Sampler preset are also retained for provenance and reference.
