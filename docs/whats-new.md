# What's new

The changes you will notice in the last few versions. The version you are using is shown in **Settings → About**.

## Version 0.22.0 (12 November 2026)

- **Your settings are remembered.** The app opens the way you left it. **Settings → About → Reset to defaults** returns everything to the starting values after asking; your own bass patterns are kept.
- **No more blank page.** If something goes wrong while the page is drawn, a recovery screen offers **Reload** and **Reset settings and reload**.
- **Status bar:** it names the open tool even when nothing is playing.
- **View switch:** Stradella, Treble and Harmony now form one switch, so they look different from the Settings and Tools buttons.
- **Settings:** every on/off preference is a checkbox.
- **Diagram margins:** the Stradella diagram always leaves room after its last button, and the row labels shrink together when one of them is long.
- **Phones:** the Fit / Readable button sits in the bottom corner of the diagram, clear of the title.
- **Faster start:** each tool's controls load the first time you open it.

## Version 0.21.0 (11 November 2026)

- **Notation position:** **Settings → Music notation → Notation position** puts every score in the right panel or below the diagram.
- **Note names below notes:** an optional setting writes the letter names under the staff.
- **Rhythm Trainer:** choose how the treble notes move, and follow the score as the notes light up.
- **Faster start:** Settings and the Rhythm Trainer load the first time you open them.
- **Help guides:** rewritten in plain language.

## Version 0.20.0 (8 November 2026)

- **New Bass–Treble Rhythm Trainer:** practice both hands together in 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8, with a timeline and a score.
- **One way of writing the left hand:** the players, sequences and the Progression Assistant write Stradella notation the same way, and Settings chooses the details: whether each chord button gets its quality symbol or only when it changes, and whether counterbass notes are marked.
- **Bass Pattern Player:** the 12/8 Shuffle, the Bo Diddley beat and the African bell pattern were corrected.
